## Caption
Every Open Volume production begins with the music and the person behind it.

Creative direction, environment, collaborators and technology should be shaped around the artist — not the other way around.

The goal is not to put artists inside an Open Volume template.

It is to give the right idea more room to become memorable.

## LinkedIn adaptation
Open Volume is artist-led by design.

The format can change — recorded, live, physical, virtual or hybrid — but the creative hierarchy should not.

Music first. Artist identity intact. Production in service of the work.
