# OV-IMG-002 — Club Intimacy

## Status
**LOCKED / APPROVED**

## Territory
Live culture / club / festival

## Purpose
- Create desire
- Demonstrate cultural credibility
- Build relationship with artists and fans

## Primary stakeholders
- Fans
- Artists
- Venues / clubs

## Approved direction
Photorealistic underground/warehouse EDM club at peak energy. Camera positioned slightly behind and beside a female DJ, with the booth embedded directly into a dense, active crowd.

## Locked qualities
- intimate artist/audience proximity
- high-density crowd
- sweat, movement, fashion, faces and social energy
- gritty warehouse architecture
- visible speaker stacks, rigging, practical fixtures and haze
- red-dominant club lighting balanced with cyan/green accents
- white light used only as secondary contrast
- credible DJ booth and equipment
- documentary immediacy rather than polished campaign staging
- no generated Open Volume branding
- no fantasy architecture

## Why it works
OV-IMG-002 provides the cultural and human counterpoint to OV-IMG-001.

- **OV-IMG-001:** destination, scale, aspiration, venue/partner value
- **OV-IMG-002:** intimacy, culture, proximity, nightlife credibility

Together they prove that Open Volume can span radically different environments without relying on one photographic treatment.
