# OV-IMG-003 — Electronic Vocalist Hero

## Status
**NEXT / IN PRODUCTION**

## Territory
Artist / editorial  
Live culture / performance

## Purpose
- Elevate the artist
- Create desire
- Demonstrate that Open Volume is artist-led rather than venue-led
- Add a strong editorial / campaign portrait to the library

## Primary stakeholders
- Artists
- Fans
- Editorial audience
- Labels / collaborators

## Scene
A hyperreal electronic vocalist in the middle of a live performance moment. The performer must feel specific, expressive and culturally current—not like a generic festival model.

The setting should be physically believable and contemporary, but the image is about the **artist**, not the stage.

## Camera concept
Medium-close cinematic portrait during performance.

Target:
- 50–85mm feel
- slightly off-axis angle
- shallow but believable depth of field
- enough environment to register the performance context
- camera close enough to capture skin texture, sweat, hair movement, expression and gesture

Avoid:
- perfectly centered beauty-ad framing
- full-body fashion pose
- anonymous silhouette
- giant stage dominating the image

## Energy
- intensity
- movement
- control
- charisma
- emotional immediacy

The artist should look like she is performing, not posing.

## Lighting
Use a third color world distinct from both OV-IMG-001 and OV-IMG-002.

Suggested:
- cobalt / deep blue base
- selective warm skin light
- ultraviolet or electric-violet accents
- one directional white source for shape
- no amber venue wash
- no red warehouse dominance

## Wardrobe
Contemporary electronic performance styling:
- distinctive but plausible
- textured technical fabrics, metallic or reflective detail where useful
- no generic cyberpunk
- no costume-like futurism
- no obvious festival cliché

## Human credibility
Prioritize:
- real skin texture
- sweat
- believable hair movement
- natural hand position
- expressive mouth/eyes
- plausible microphone grip
- real body mechanics
- subtle imperfections

## Environment
Believable live venue or controlled performance stage:
- LED/projection surfaces may appear out of focus
- haze may be present
- no impossible architecture
- no fantasy world
- no invented brand logos

## Brand presence
**None in source image.**
If used as a campaign or website hero, actual Open Volume identity can be applied in layout/post.

## Exclusions
- generic pop-star glamour shot
- stock concert photo
- cyberpunk costume
- fantasy venue
- fake logos
- luminous-ring motif
- plastic skin
- over-smoothed face
- exaggerated AI hair
- all-white lighting
- amber-heavy grading
- red-dominant club lighting

## Intended uses
- Stories hero
- homepage artist section
- social campaign
- editorial feature
- launch deck
- artist-facing materials
- 4:5 social
- 9:16 poster
- 16:9 crop if composition allows
