# OV-IMG-004 — Premium Club Crowd Culture

## Status
**LOCKED / APPROVED**

## Territory
Audience / culture  
Premium nightclub / electronic music

## Purpose
- Build belonging
- Demonstrate nightlife credibility
- Show the audience as part of the Open Volume cultural proposition
- Provide venue/partner-facing social proof without turning the image into a stage shot

## Approved direction
A premium, large-format nightclub environment with layered architecture, balcony depth, visible DJ/stage orientation, and a dense, well-dressed electronic-music crowd.

## Locked qualities
- spatially coherent DJ/stage placement
- crowd behavior distributed naturally across watching, dancing, talking, drinking, and filming
- premium-club dress-code credibility
- varied hair, grooming, styling, skin tones, and individual presentation
- sunglasses used sparingly
- no hats or casual styling that would undermine a premium-club environment
- fewer repeated raised-finger gestures
- hands/gestures must read naturally and pass anatomy QA
- blue/violet/cyan lighting with restrained warm practicals
- crowd reads as an ecosystem rather than a row of posed hero subjects
- no fantasy environment
- no generated Open Volume branding
