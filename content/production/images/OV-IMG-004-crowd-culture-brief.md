# OV-IMG-004 — Crowd Culture

## Status
**NEXT / IN PRODUCTION**

## Territory
Audience / culture

## Purpose
- Build belonging
- Demonstrate cultural credibility
- Show Open Volume understands the audience beyond the stage
- Reduce over-reliance on performer-centric imagery

## Primary stakeholders
- Fans
- Artists
- Cultural partners
- Venues

## Scene
A believable electronic-music crowd moment where the audience itself is the subject.

The image should feel like a photographer found a great social moment inside an active event rather than a staged lifestyle shoot.

## Camera concept
Medium-close documentary frame inside the crowd.

Target:
- 35–50mm feel
- shoulder/chest height
- several people in foreground/midground
- enough depth to communicate a live environment
- asymmetrical composition
- partial occlusion is welcome
- one or two strong faces, not a wall of equally sharp people

## Energy
- joy
- movement
- friendship
- attraction
- release
- confidence
- being part of a scene

Avoid reducing the crowd to anonymous hands in the air.

## Casting
Intentional diversity in appearance, styling and body language.

Avoid:
- repeating the same female-artist archetype
- MAEVRA-adjacent casting
- all subjects looking like models from the same campaign
- over-styled festival costume clichés

## Lighting
Fourth distinct visual territory.

Suggested:
- saturated but natural club/festival color
- electric violet and cyan with occasional warm practical light
- mixed-source lighting that feels physically real
- faces should remain readable

Avoid:
- red-dominant warehouse lighting from OV-IMG-002
- amber sunset from OV-IMG-001
- cobalt artist-lighting from OV-IMG-003 as the only source
- white-beam-heavy stage lighting

## Environment
Believable club, festival side-stage, or cultural venue setting.

The environment should register through:
- haze
- distant practicals
- blurred stage/venue cues
- people behind the subjects

No fantasy architecture.

## Brand presence
**None in source image.**

## Exclusions
- stock “festival girls smiling at camera”
- posed influencer group
- giant stage dominating frame
- generic hands-up crowd
- invented logos
- obvious AI faces/hands
- one-note color wash
- identical fashion/styling across subjects

## Intended uses
- homepage audience section
- Stories
- social
- launch campaign
- partner decks
- culture/editorial posts
