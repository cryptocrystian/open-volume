# OV-IMG-002 — Club Intimacy

## Status
**NEXT / IN PRODUCTION**

## Territory
Live culture / club / festival

## Purpose
- Create desire
- Demonstrate cultural credibility
- Build relationship with artists and fans

## Primary stakeholders
- Fans
- Artists
- Venues / clubs

## Scene
A physically believable underground or warehouse electronic club at peak energy. DJ booth embedded close to the audience rather than elevated far away. Dense crowd, real sweat, fashion, movement, faces, arms, friends dancing together, and genuine artist/audience proximity.

## Camera concept
Camera positioned slightly behind and to the side of the DJ, around shoulder height, with a 24–35mm documentary/cinematic feel. DJ and controls remain partially in foreground while the crowd dominates the emotional center.

Avoid symmetry. Avoid a giant festival-stage composition.

## Energy
Immediate, kinetic, intimate, social, sweaty, current.

## Lighting
Use a deliberately different color world from OV-IMG-001.

Target:
- saturated red as the dominant practical/stage light
- acid green or cyan accents
- occasional white strobe only as a secondary source
- dark ceiling, haze, reflections, practical fixtures

Avoid:
- amber sunset warmth
- white beams as the dominant visual
- generic blue-magenta EDM grading

## Human credibility
- varied fashion and body language
- real skin texture
- believable crowd density
- people dancing with each other, not only facing the stage
- DJ visibly engaged with the room
- no cloned faces, duplicated gestures, or anatomically strange hands

## Venue
Believable warehouse / underground club architecture:
- exposed beams
- low or medium ceiling
- speakers
- practical lighting
- rigging
- imperfect walls/floors
- no fantasy architecture

## Brand presence
**None in source generation.**
Real Open Volume identity, if desired, will be added later to a plausible LED screen, booth fascia, or projection in post.

## Exclusions
- invented logo
- glowing ring motif
- fantasy venue
- immaculate luxury club
- generic stock crowd
- arena-scale perspective
- anonymous silhouetted DJ
- all-white lighting
- amber-heavy grade
- obvious AI texture
