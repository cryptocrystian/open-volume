Four 12-frame proof rounds were used to establish the visual direction. The resulting boards are internal calibration references, not a final public asset set.

## Locked conclusions

### Keep as reference territories
- cinematic destination drone photography
- intimate club/DJ proximity
- electronic vocalist hero frames
- vibrant crowd culture
- festival scale
- contemporary / culturally interesting venues
- artist editorial portraiture
- backstage/process
- recorded/studio performance
- architecture and place
- believable spatial media inside real environments

### Do not use as launch master-brand territory
- impossible/fantasy environments
- floating worlds
- synthetic monumental architecture
- fantasy venues
- generic sci-fi
- repeated luminous rings/portals

These may be retained separately as possible World 01 exploration references.

### Brand rule
Do not generate Open Volume identity inside source imagery.

Apply the approved production identity selectively in post.
