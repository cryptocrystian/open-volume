# Open Volume — Launch Content Production Board

## Production rule

Every asset must primarily do at least one of four jobs:

1. **Create desire**
2. **Demonstrate taste**
3. **Reveal capability**
4. **Build relationship**

Across the full launch body, Open Volume must visibly communicate artistic, creative, cultural and commercial value.
