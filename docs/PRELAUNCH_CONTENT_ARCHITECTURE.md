# Open Volume — 90-Day Pre-Launch Content Architecture

## Purpose
Build recognition, taste, trust, and anticipation before the first flagship experience without turning Open Volume into a startup-build-in-public feed.

Every piece of content should primarily do one or more of four jobs:

1. **Create desire** — make the audience want to experience Open Volume.
2. **Demonstrate taste** — show cultural understanding and editorial judgment.
3. **Reveal capability** — provide credible evidence that Open Volume can execute ambitious work.
4. **Build relationship** — give fans, artists, venues, collaborators, and partners a reason to stay close.

Across the body of content, the brand must consistently communicate **artistic, creative, cultural, and commercial value**.

## Phase 1 — Establish the signal / Weeks 1–3
Goal: establish identity, point of view, and cultural territory before explaining every capability.

Initial assets:
1. Open Volume identity introduction.
2. **Expand the space music can occupy.** brand film.
3. Brand-thesis carousel: physical / creative / cultural / digital / emotional space.
4. Artist-first philosophy: **The artist leads. The format follows.**
5. Audience proposition: experiences with intention, not feed filler.
6. Open Volume Stories introduction.

## Phase 2 — Demonstrate taste / Weeks 4–6
Goal: begin behaving like a cultural property.

7. First **Close Listening** artist conversation or profile.
8. First **In Place** feature on a venue, city, destination, or environment where place affects the experience.
9. First **Perspective** essay or short film.
10. First **Discovery** feature with a genuine editorial reason for selection.
11. **Process Notes — Lighting**.

## Phase 3 — Reveal capability / Weeks 7–9
Goal: show craft before the blueprint.

12. **Process Notes — Camera / Movement**.
13. Controlled production fragment.
14. Creative-collaborator feature.
15. Spatial, sound-response, or environmental study approved for controlled release.

Reveal fragments of material, light, movement, camera language, rehearsal, production craft, and collaboration. Do not publish full unapproved world layouts, production architecture diagrams, Unreal-development screens as marketing, or material that prematurely defines World 01.

## Phase 4 — Build anticipation / Weeks 10–12
Goal: bridge from brand-building into the first approved Open Volume experience.

16. **Something is taking shape** teaser / owned-audience capture.
17. Approved location/world fragment.
18. Artist tease or announcement when cleared.
19. Experience announcement.
20. Artist story / why the collaboration exists.
21. Venue or environment reveal.
22. Controlled behind-the-experience story.
23. Countdown / premiere.
24. Post-event continuation and archive.

## Initial channel roles
- **Instagram** — primary visual expression, artist announcements, carousels, Reels, editorial, place, and controlled development signals.
- **YouTube** — canonical home for substantial performances, Sessions, films, conversations, and long-lived media.
- **TikTok** — discovery-led artist moments, musical hooks, exceptional visual moments, and concise process storytelling without generic trend-chasing.
- **LinkedIn** — industry, venue, destination, label, collaborator, and commercial-partner credibility.
- **Email / Stay Close** — owned audience for meaningful announcements and editorial, event-driven rather than volume-driven.

## Reuse doctrine
One strong production asset should become a cross-channel system rather than isolated posts.

Example: **Close Listening**
- full Story page
- video or long-form conversation
- social carousel
- short video excerpts
- email feature
- artist amplification assets

Example: first experience
- Experience page
- trailer
- artist feature
- venue/world story
- short-form clips
- still photography
- editorial extensions
- partner amplification
- premiere
- archive
