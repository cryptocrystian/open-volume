# Open Volume — Image Production Brief Template

Use one brief per production image or tightly related image family.

---

## Asset ID
OV-IMG-___

## Working title
Short descriptive title.

## Territory
Choose one:
- Live culture / club / festival
- Artist / editorial
- Place / destination / architecture
- Audience / culture
- Process / backstage / rehearsal
- Recorded / cinematic / studio
- Believable hybrid / spatial media
- Hero-brand

## Purpose
What job does the image perform?

Choose one or more:
- Create desire
- Demonstrate taste
- Reveal capability
- Build relationship

## Primary stakeholder
- Fan
- Artist
- Venue / destination
- Brand / sponsor
- Creative collaborator
- Industry

## Intended placements
Examples:
- Homepage hero
- Homepage section
- Experiences
- Stories
- About
- Partners
- Instagram 4:5
- Reel/TikTok poster
- YouTube
- LinkedIn
- Email
- Deck / partner material

## Scene
Describe the real-world situation.

Keep it physically plausible unless this is explicitly an approved World 01 asset.

## Subject
Who or what is the main subject?

Avoid anonymous “festival person” unless audience culture itself is the subject.

## Energy
Specify:
- anticipation
- intimacy
- movement
- peak release
- concentration
- joy
- scale
- afterglow
- tension
- calm

## Environment
Specify:
- venue/location type
- architecture
- geography
- crowd density
- time of day
- weather/atmosphere if relevant

## Camera concept
Specify intentionally:
- drone height/angle
- lens range
- camera height
- distance
- POV
- handheld/static
- depth of field
- motion blur
- symmetry/asymmetry
- negative-space requirement

## Lighting
Describe the source and logic.

Examples:
- practical red + green club lighting
- hard daylight
- blue-hour ambient + venue practicals
- sodium-vapor street light
- authored white spotlight + saturated LED spill
- neutral studio/rehearsal working light

Do not say only “cinematic lighting.”

## Color territory
Describe the desired image palette without forcing Open Volume brand colors into the photograph.

## Human credibility notes
Specify:
- skin texture
- sweat
- fashion
- gesture
- eye line
- believable crowd behavior
- performer body mechanics

## Open Volume brand presence

Choose one:
- None
- Subtle environmental
- Hero brand

If branded, define the exact real-world surface where the approved identity will be composited in post.

**Do not generate the logo in-image.**

## Exclusions
List what this particular image must avoid.

Common:
- fantasy architecture
- invented logos
- portal/ring motif
- blue-magenta default grade
- generic hands-in-air composition
- empty AI spectacle
- duplicated faces
- impossible anatomy
- fake text/signage
- generic metaverse imagery

## Crop requirements
Check required outputs:
- [ ] 16:9
- [ ] 4:5
- [ ] 1:1
- [ ] 9:16
- [ ] ultra-wide
- [ ] text-safe negative space

## Approval criteria
The image is approved only if:
1. it is photographically believable
2. it contributes a distinct visual territory
3. it fits Open Volume without defining the entire brand
4. it serves the intended stakeholder/purpose
5. no invented brand identity is present
6. it survives intended crops
7. it feels culturally credible rather than generic stock
