# Open Volume — Social Profile Launch Pack v1

## Principle
Social should make Open Volume feel like a cultural property worth following before the first major production is publicly announced.

Do not position the accounts as:
- a production-services company
- an Unreal/AI showcase
- a metaverse project
- a generic EDM repost page

## Display name
**Open Volume**

Use the plain master brand wherever available.

## Handle strategy
Preferred order:
1. @openvolume
2. @open.volume
3. @openvolume.music
4. a similarly restrained modifier only if required

Do not add “official,” “media,” “studios,” “world,” or technology language unless forced by availability.

## Instagram / TikTok bio
**Expand the space music can occupy.**  
Performances, experiences + culture.

Alternate expanded version:
**Music + culture. Original performances, experiences and collaborations across physical, virtual + hybrid spaces.**

## YouTube description
Open Volume creates and curates original performances, experiences and collaborations across physical, virtual and hybrid spaces.

Music leads. Format follows.

## LinkedIn company description
Open Volume is a music and culture platform creating original performances, experiences and collaborations across physical, virtual and hybrid spaces.

We work with artists, venues, destinations, brands and creative collaborators to expand the space music can occupy.

## Profile-image direction
Use the canonical standalone Resonance 01 symbol from the approved production master.

Do not:
- recreate it in live type or CSS
- use an invented ring mark
- use generated logo artwork
- crop a temporary screenshot as the permanent avatar

## Banner / channel-art direction
Use one of:
- a striking approved photographic crop with restrained typography
- Charcoal/Bone brand composition
- campaign image once a real Open Volume production is announced

Avoid using fantasy World 01 imagery before World 01 is actually ready to reveal.

## Link destination
At launch, social profiles should point to the homepage unless a live campaign page has a stronger reason to become the primary destination.

## Launch sequence
Publish enough material that a new visitor understands Open Volume within the first screenful of content.

Recommended first 9 posts:

1. **Brand introduction** — Open Volume / Expand the space music can occupy.
2. **Destination / scale image** — show ambition and place.
3. **Artist-led philosophy** — The artist leads. The format follows.
4. **Club intimacy image** — cultural credibility and proximity.
5. **Perspective 01** — What makes a music experience worth remembering?
6. **Electronic vocalist hero** — artist-centered visual.
7. **Stories introduction** — Artists / Places / Process / Perspective / Discovery.
8. **Premium crowd culture** — belonging and scene credibility.
9. **Something is taking shape** — controlled anticipation + Stay Close CTA.

## Posting rhythm
Do not dump all nine at once.

Initial recommendation:
- establish 3 posts before actively driving traffic
- build to 6 within the first 7–10 days
- reach 9 within roughly 2–3 weeks
- layer Stories/Reels/Shorts between permanent-feed posts

Quality and cultural relevance are more important than daily volume.

## Channel role
### Instagram
Primary visual expression and cultural front door.

### TikTok
Discovery, performance fragments, artist moments, concise editorial/process.

### YouTube
Canonical long-form performances, films, Sessions, conversations and durable archive.

### LinkedIn
Venue, partner, collaborator and industry credibility without becoming corporate content.

## Non-negotiable
The accounts must not imply announced artists, venues, sponsors, events or partnerships that have not actually been approved.
