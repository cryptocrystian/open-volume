## Cross-channel adaptation
### Instagram
Canonical visual expression. Use 4:5 when practical.

### TikTok
Motion-first adaptations; avoid publishing static carousel logic without adding movement/audio value.

### YouTube
Shorts for launch signals; long-form reserved for substantial performances, conversations, and films.

### LinkedIn
Adapt the underlying idea for artists, venues, collaborators, and partners. Do not simply repost Instagram captions verbatim when an industry framing would be stronger.

## Launch rule
No post may imply a confirmed artist, venue, sponsor, destination, or public event unless that relationship is real and cleared for publication.
