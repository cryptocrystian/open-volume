# Open Volume — Launch Readiness Checklist v2

## Launch objective

Move Open Volume from a complete launch shell into a credible public cultural property with enough owned media, channel infrastructure, and launch content to begin building audience immediately.

Open Volume is **not** being launched as an event promoter. The public launch must communicate the full model:

**Production Platform / Engine → Media & Cultural Property → Experiences → Commercial Rails**

Electronic music is the explicit cultural center. Live events are one manifestation of the platform, not the company itself.

Open Volume does **not** need a full 60-image library, sponsor roster, ticketing stack, festival plan, or mature Unreal world catalog before launch.

## 2. Launch media library

### Launch quantity target
**12–18 genuinely approved stills before launch.**

Do not produce filler to reach a number. Every asset must have a defined placement or launch-content role.

### Final brand position
Imagery should express **elevated electronic culture**:

- premium execution
- cultural credibility
- music first
- aspiration without elitism
- sophistication without sterility
- human energy without grunge as the master identity
- physically believable production
- enough visual range to support physical, recorded, virtual, hybrid, and future live formats

### Sonic center of gravity
Launch-era artist/music imagery should broadly support:

**Core:** melodic house & techno, progressive house, deep house, Afro house, selected organic house  
**Adjacent:** tech house, indie dance, classic house, sophisticated techno, progressive trance  
**Edge:** garage, drum & bass, harder techno, experimental and cross-genre work

This is a programming center, not a public genre fence.

### Required launch asset set

#### A. Elevated performance culture — 4–5
- [ ] intimate premium club / artist-audience proximity
- [ ] architecturally distinctive performance
- [ ] producer / DJ performance with non-default lighting
- [ ] vocalist + electronic performance
- [ ] one larger-scale physically credible flagship frame

#### B. Audience / culture — 2–3
- [ ] emotionally alive intimate crowd
- [ ] larger crowd treated as density/motion rather than wall-of-faces
- [ ] stylish social/cultural moment that remains music-first

#### C. Artist / editorial — 2–3
- [ ] artist portrait
- [ ] candid/preparation frame
- [ ] collaborator / musician interaction

#### D. Process / platform breadth — 2–3
- [ ] rehearsal or soundcheck
- [ ] lighting/camera/FOH/production process
- [ ] recorded/cinematic/studio setup

#### E. Place / architecture — 1–2
- [ ] venue/place with real musical relevance
- [ ] optional destination/architectural hero without resort-advertising drift

#### F. Brand hero — 1
- [ ] Open Volume master-brand hero with real identity applied in post

### Mandatory media QA
Every approved launch asset must pass:

- [ ] 3-second electronic-music category test
- [ ] physically plausible venue / rig / equipment / camera position
- [ ] no repeated or deformed crowd faces
- [ ] no robotic audience lines
- [ ] crowd behavior shows varied emotion and social behavior
- [ ] eyelines / orientation make spatial sense
- [ ] hands/anatomy survive 200% zoom
- [ ] no accidental MAEVRA resemblance unless explicitly intended
- [ ] no invented logos or fake readable signage
- [ ] no recurring amber/golden-hour default
- [ ] no recurring red-only club default
- [ ] no recurring white-beam / blue-white haze default
- [ ] no repeated generic brunette female performer archetype
- [ ] adds a visual territory not already overused
- [ ] final retouch completed before approval
- [ ] intended crops tested

Raw AI generation is never final by default.
