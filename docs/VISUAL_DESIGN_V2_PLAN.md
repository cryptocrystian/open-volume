# Open Volume — Visual Design v2

Status: implementation pass on `website-messaging-v2`

## Objective
Move the public site from a clean editorial scaffold to a recognizable electronic-music and culture property. The website should feel cinematic, selective, artist-led and production-serious without becoming a nightclub template, festival microsite, tech demo or generic luxury destination brand.

## Homepage design principles
- Lead with a cinematic performance image rather than a text-only shell.
- Use the locked Open Volume identity as the visual anchor; imagery carries atmosphere, not brand geometry.
- Break the repeated two-column section rhythm with full-bleed media, editorial offsets, oversized statements and quieter negative-space sections.
- Keep electronic-music cues explicit but restrained: artist/audience relationship, production hardware, light, crowd energy and real staging.
- Preserve shadow and materiality. Avoid white-beam-heavy EDM cliches, neon cyberpunk, glassmorphism and excessive UI-like cards.
- Audience sections should feel open and participatory; partner sections should feel selective and controlled.
- Mobile is composed independently: imagery crops, type scale, order and spacing must remain intentional rather than merely stacking desktop columns.

## Homepage rhythm
1. Cinematic media hero with strong category clarity.
2. Light manifesto / production thesis.
3. Artist-led editorial feature.
4. Audience section with stronger visual immersion.
5. Full-width Open Volume Presents feature.
6. Stories / culture editorial module.
7. Selective partners module.
8. Screen / Stage / Real Life platform statement.
9. High-contrast audience capture close.

## Media direction
The first hero should use the approved launch visual language: cinematic environmental scale, unmistakable electronic-performance cues, integrated violet/teal color spill, restrained practical lighting and physically plausible production detail.

## Guardrails
- No generated or approximate Open Volume marks.
- No literal World 01 visual vocabulary in launch brand media.
- No MAEVRA-coded casting unless MAEVRA is explicitly featured.
- No giant ring structures used as a recurring stage motif.
- No stock-style crowd + laser imagery as the main creative idea.
