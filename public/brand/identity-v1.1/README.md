# Open Volume — Identity Kit v1.1
Production release · 24 September 2026

Start with docs/Identity_Preview.png and docs/Open_Volume_Design_System_v1.1.html.

## Identity
Approved Resonance 01 mark + modern B typography direction. Two closed rings and solid center. Vector symbol reconstructed and smoothed from approved artwork. Wordmark is an Outfit Regular-derived outlined treatment (spacing and proportions customized to B); it requires no font installation. Outfit supports editable typography throughout the brand.

## Files
- svg/: Symbol, Wordmark, Stacked, Horizontal in Charcoal, Bone, Black, White, Mineral, Oxide and Recolorable (currentColor).
- pdf/: vector equivalents for fixed colors. Light-color PDFs have transparent pages and may appear blank on white viewers; inspect against dark background. These are artwork masters, not press-ready imposed layouts.
- png/: transparent RGBA logos at 512 and 2048 px width.
- avatars/: square SVG and 1024 px PNG in Dark, Light and Oxide.
- favicon/: SVG, ICO (16/32/48/64), PNG 16/24/32/48/64/180/192/512, webmanifest.
- fonts/: variable TTF + WOFF2, original SIL OFL license and source attribution.
- templates/: editable SVG shells for cover, portrait, story, video thumbnail and social header. Replace the marked artwork group and live titles. Install Outfit for editing; the embedded webfont supports capable renderers, but editor support varies. Re-export after editing and inspect typography.
- web/: CSS tokens, JSON tokens and favicon HTML snippet. Paths assume folders retained; adjust when integrating.
- source/: approved concept boards and vector path data. Reference images are NOT production logos.
- docs/: system, preview, small-size proof and QA report.

## Usage
Prefer SVG. Keep proportions and negative-space channels intact. Logo variants have only modest internal export padding: add external clear space of at least one quarter of symbol height. Never crop to the SVG boundary for signage.
Recolor the entire logo together for world/partner contexts. Recolorable SVG uses currentColor when inline; if used as an img file, set its fill explicitly or use a fixed-color export.
Worlds have independent identities; this system governs Open Volume assets and its brand signature.

## Minimums
Routine standalone symbol: 32 CSS px wide; lockups: 160 px wide. Small favicon exports are compatibility fallbacks, not permission to use tiny logos in marketing. Print starting floors: symbol 10 mm; lockups 40 mm, supplier-proofed. Same geometry is used for positive and reverse versions.

## Remaining medium-specific work
Digital files are delivered. Metal cutting, illuminated signs, embroidery, print color conversion and stage LED reproduction require proofs for the chosen supplier/material. Motion and sonic identity production are separate future deliverables. No trademark clearance or uniqueness guarantee is implied by this kit.
